About this Network

This map shows how University of Idaho academic units depend on each other's courses to satisfy degree requirements. It is designed for curriculum-review conversations: where are the load-bearing dependencies, and which units quietly anchor the curriculum?

Reading the graph

  • Nodes are academic units (departments/schools), colored by college. Node size ∝ total incident requirement weight (dependencies placed on the unit plus dependencies it places on others).
  • Edges are directed: degree-owning unit → course-owning unit. Thickness ∝ number of required courses drawn from the target unit. Arrowheads point at the unit whose courses are consumed.
  • Core only counts required + choice-list courses. Core + electives also counts curated elective menus.

Interactions

  • Hover nodes/edges for detail; click a node or edge to open the detail panel and highlight its neighborhood.
  • Drag nodes to pin them (sticky). Reset view clears pins and filters; double-click the background re-fits.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag background to pan. Use weighting, min-weight, and college chips to filter.

Method

Requirements were parsed from the 2026–2027 catalog curriculum pages. Course lists are grouped per edge into required, choice (one-of sets), and elective menu contexts. Self-loops (courses from the owning unit) are excluded.
